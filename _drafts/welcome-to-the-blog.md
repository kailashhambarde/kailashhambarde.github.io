This blog will collect short notes from my research work in computer vision, person re-identification, and applied machine learning.

I plan to use this space for practical reflections that do not always fit into a paper: dataset lessons, experimental design notes, reading lists, challenge summaries, and implementation details from projects involving aerial-ground video, extreme-distance recognition, and robust visual analysis.

The first goal is simple: keep useful research thinking visible, searchable, and easy to share.
